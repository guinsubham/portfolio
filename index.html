// About Me

Where filmmaking
meets AI

I'm a video editor and motion graphics designer with more than six years of experience across film, television, advertising, and digital content. What sets me apart is how deeply I integrate generative AI throughout the production process.

I don't just edit videos; I build AI-powered workflows that take projects from initial ideas to final delivery. Whether I'm using AI to create ads with consistent characters and products, generating content at scale, or building custom GPTs and Claude skills to automate creative tasks, I work at the intersection of traditional craft and emerging technology.

At Meraaquii D&C, I lead video editing and motion graphics while driving AI adoption throughout the production pipeline for major Indian brands.
