Where filmmaking meets AI

I'm a Video Editor and Motion Graphics Designer with 6+ years of experience spanning film, television, advertising, and digital content. What sets me apart is my deep integration of Generative AI into every stage of production.

I don't just edit videos — I build AI-powered workflows that take a project from idea to final video.
